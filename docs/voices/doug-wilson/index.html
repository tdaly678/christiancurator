Doug Wilson

Senior minister of Christ Church, Moscow, Idaho | Blog and Mablog at dougwils.com | Writes on covenant theology, worship, classical Christian education and the Christian life

About

Douglas Wilson is senior minister of Christ Church in Moscow, Idaho, where he has served as a pastor since 1977, and he blogs at Blog and Mablog (dougwils.com). After serving in the U.S. Navy submarine service, he earned two bachelor's degrees, in philosophy and classical studies, and a master's in philosophy from the University of Idaho. New Saint Andrews College lists him among the founders of the college, Canon Press and the Communion of Reformed Evangelical Churches; he was a founding board member of Logos School and is a senior fellow at New Saint Andrews. His books include Recovering the Lost Tools of Learning (Crossway, 1991) and the novel Evangellyfish.

Source: About page, dougwils.com · Contributor page, New Saint Andrews College · Leadership and staff, Christ Church. Last updated November 12, 2026.

  1. Objections to Election
    Tabletalk · 2001-03

    Argues that the objection in Romans 9:19 applies to any orthodox view of God, not only to Reformed theology, and that its answer lies in God's authority as Creator.

  2. Humility Properly Defined
    Tabletalk · 2001-02

    Reads the Beatitudes to argue that humility begins with recognizing one's spiritual need before a sovereign and gracious God and accepting salvation on his terms.

  3. Worship Is Warfare
    Tabletalk · 2001-01

    Argues that the church advances Christ's rule chiefly through its weekly worship, preaching and sacraments, rather than through political lobbying or a purely private faith.

  4. Christ the Lord of Covenantal Partaking
    Blog and Mablog · February 22, 2008

    Sets out a case from 1 Corinthians 10 and 11 for weekly communion and for Christ's covenantal presence in the Supper for those who partake in faith, in conversation with Calvin and the Westminster Confession.
