Alex Harris

Attorney and Keller Center fellow | Coauthor of Do Hard Things | Writes for The Gospel Coalition on faith, mortality, and public life

About

Alex Harris is an attorney and a fellow at The Keller Center for Cultural Apologetics. As a teenager he coauthored Do Hard Things: A Teenage Rebellion Against Low Expectations with his twin brother, Brett. A graduate of Harvard Law School, he worked as a law clerk for U.S. Supreme Court Justices Neil Gorsuch and Anthony Kennedy.

Source: Profile, The Gospel Coalition · Fellows, The Keller Center. Last updated November 12, 2026.
