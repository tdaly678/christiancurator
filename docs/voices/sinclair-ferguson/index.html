Sinclair B. Ferguson

Ligonier teaching fellow and Chancellor's Professor of Systematic Theology at Reformed Theological Seminary | Author of The Whole Christ | Writes on sanctification, law and grace, suffering and the Reformation

About

Sinclair B. Ferguson is a teaching fellow and vice-chairman of Ligonier Ministries, Chancellor's Professor of Systematic Theology at Reformed Theological Seminary and host of the podcast Things Unseen. He received his PhD from the University of Aberdeen in 1971. He served Church of Scotland congregations in Unst, Shetland, and at St George's Tron in Glasgow, held the Charles Krahe chair of systematic theology at Westminster Theological Seminary, and retired in 2013 as senior minister of First Presbyterian Church in Columbia, South Carolina. His books include The Whole Christ, Maturity, Devoted to God's Church and By Grace Alone.

Start here

  1. Learning Contentment
    Ligonier Ministries · January 13, 2023

    Argues that contentment cannot be reached by steps or willpower and is a Christlike character God forms through teaching and experience, especially adversity.

  2. Who Was John Knox?
    Ligonier Ministries · February 25, 2014

    Traces Knox from galley slave and royal chaplain in England through exile in Geneva to his return to lead the Reformation in Scotland, and argues that his sufferings, experience and sense of call fitted him for that work.

  3. The Surprising Truth about Legalism
    Crossway · January 19, 2016

    Traces legalism to a distorted view of God as withholding, going back to Eden, and argues that antinomianism grows from the same root and that only the gospel answers both.

  4. How God Uses Friction to Shape Us
    Crossway · May 10, 2023

    Reads Romans 5 to argue that God uses sustained hardship to produce endurance, tested character and hope in believers.
