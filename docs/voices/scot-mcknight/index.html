Scot McKnight

New Testament Scholar & Author

Scot McKnight is an acclaimed New Testament scholar and historian of early Christianity who has written over eighty books on the historical Jesus, Christian theology, and biblical interpretation. A leading voice in evangelical scholarship, his work has shaped conversations on scripture, the kingdom of God, and ecclesiology.
