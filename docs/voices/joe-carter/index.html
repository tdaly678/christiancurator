Joe Carter

About

Joe Carter is a contributing editor for The Gospel Coalition and an associate pastor at McLean Bible Church in Arlington, Virginia. He holds an MDiv from Midwestern Baptist Theological Seminary. He is the author of The Life and Faith Field Guide for Parents, editor of the NIV Spiritual Habits Bible and coauthor of How to Argue Like Jesus: Learning Persuasion from History's Greatest Communicator.

Source: Profile, The Gospel Coalition · Author bio, The Gospel Coalition · Author page, Crossway. Last updated November 12, 2026.
