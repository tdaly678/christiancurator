Christina Grube

Breaking News Reporter, WORLD

Christina Grube is a breaking news reporter for WORLD News Group, the Christian journalism organization behind WORLD Magazine. A graduate of the World Journalism Institute, she writes daily for The Sift, WORLD's morning news briefing, covering religious liberty cases, national politics, and breaking stories from a biblically grounded newsroom.
