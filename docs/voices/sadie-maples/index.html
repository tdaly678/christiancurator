Sadie Maples

Editorial Fellow, Christianity Today

Sadie Maples is the 2026 Gene Habecker Fellow at Christianity Today, where she writes on Christian living, spiritual formation, and contemporary culture. Her essays include reflections on why mindfulness alone cannot satisfy, the church's calling amid America's 250th anniversary, and how Christ's message bridges the country's deepest social divides.
