James Abernathy

Attorney in First Amendment litigation | Writes for The Gospel Coalition and on Substack on disability, the body, and the dignity of dependence

About

James Abernathy is an attorney who specializes in litigation defending First Amendment rights. He holds an MA from Fuller Theological Seminary and a JD from Regent University School of Law. A quadriplegic since a spinal cord injury in high school, he writes and speaks to advocate for the body and the dignity of dependence.

Source: Profile, The Gospel Coalition · Author note, The Gospel Coalition · About page, James Abernathy (Substack). Last updated November 12, 2026.

  1. Why I'm a Disabled Person, Not a Person with a Disability
    The Gospel Coalition · March 31, 2026

    Argues that the body is part of a person's identity as God's image bearer, and so rejects the person-first language that separates dignity from disability.

  2. Broken Bodies Make Beautiful Temples
    The Gospel Coalition · October 25, 2026

    Argues from the body as a temple of the Holy Spirit that bodies marked by disability or illness belong to God and should be trusted to his purposes, not remade by the self.

  3. My Disabled Body is My Forever Body (Being God's Image, Part V)
    Substack · June 15, 2026

    Argues that God will raise and glorify the same bodies believers have now, so the present body, including its disability, should be honored rather than despised.

  4. Will There Be Disability at the Resurrection? Part 1: Six Guiding Principles
    Substack · August 6, 2026

    Sets out six scriptural principles for asking whether disability remains after the resurrection, beginning with competing definitions of disability.
