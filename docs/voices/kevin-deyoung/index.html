Kevin DeYoung

Senior pastor of Christ Covenant Church since 2017 and associate professor at Reformed Theological Seminary | Author of The Hole in Our Holiness and Taking God at His Word | Writes at Clearly Reformed and The Gospel Coalition on doctrine, the church and politics

About

Kevin DeYoung has been senior pastor of Christ Covenant Church in Matthews, North Carolina, since 2017 and is associate professor of systematic theology at Reformed Theological Seminary in Charlotte. He was previously senior pastor of University Reformed Church in East Lansing, Michigan, and was elected moderator of the PCA's 52nd General Assembly in 2025. He holds a BA from Hope College, an MDiv from Gordon-Conwell Theological Seminary and a PhD in early modern history from the University of Leicester. His books include Just Do Something, The Hole in Our Holiness, Taking God at His Word, Daily Doctrine and The Biggest Story.

Source: About Kevin DeYoung, Clearly Reformed · Faculty page, Reformed Theological Seminary · Staff page, Christ Covenant Church. Last updated November 12, 2026.
