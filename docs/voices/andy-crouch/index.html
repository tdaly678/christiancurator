Andy Crouch

Partner for theology and culture at Praxis | Author of Culture Making and The Tech-Wise Family | Writes on power, technology, culture and institutions

About

Andy Crouch is partner for theology and culture at Praxis and a teaching fellow for Practicing the Way. He was an editor at Christianity Today for more than ten years, serving as executive editor from 2012 to 2016, and earlier was a campus minister with InterVarsity Christian Fellowship at Harvard University. He studied classics at Cornell University and holds an MDiv from Boston University School of Theology. His books include Culture Making (2008), Playing God (2013), Strong and Weak (2016), The Tech-Wise Family (2017) and The Life We're Looking For (2022).

Source: Bio, andy-crouch.com · Team profile, Praxis. Last updated November 13, 2026.

Start here

  1. It's Time to Reckon with Celebrity Power
    andy-crouch.com (first published by The Gospel Coalition) · March 24, 2018

    Argues that celebrity pairs the distance of power with a false sense of intimacy that lets leaders escape accountability, and calls churches to strengthen institutions and limit personal power.

  2. It's Time to Talk About Power
    andy-crouch.com (first published in Christianity Today) · 2013

    Argues that power is a gift meant for the flourishing of others, that it is harder to hold accountable when it goes unacknowledged, and that Scripture joins power and servanthood.

  3. Stop Engaging 'The Culture'
    andy-crouch.com (first published in Christianity Today) · 2016

    Argues that 'the culture' is too vague to engage and that Christian influence comes from loving particular neighbors in particular places and building alternatives, as the early church did.

  4. Small Screens, Big World
    andy-crouch.com · April 8, 2015

    Reflects on giving up screens for Lent and argues that devices make people less attentive to the world, which embodied experience reveals in ways a screen cannot.
