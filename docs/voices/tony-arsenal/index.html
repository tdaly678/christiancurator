Tony Arsenal

Writer

Tony Arsenal writes The Reformed Arsenal, a theological blog on Reformed doctrine and biblical exposition. According to the blog's About page, he is a seminary trained layperson with master's degrees in church history and theology, a member of an Orthodox Presbyterian Church, and occasionally provides pulpit supply at local churches.
