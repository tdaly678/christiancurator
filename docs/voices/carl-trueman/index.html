Carl R. Trueman

Professor of biblical and religious studies at Grove City College | Author of The Rise and Triumph of the Modern Self | Writes on the modern self, the church and evangelical identity

About

Carl R. Trueman is professor of biblical and religious studies at Grove City College and a fellow at the Ethics and Public Policy Center. He holds a PhD from the University of Aberdeen, is a church historian and is a contributing editor at First Things. He has authored or edited more than a dozen books, including The Rise and Triumph of the Modern Self, Strange New World and Histories and Fallacies, and is a member of the Orthodox Presbyterian Church.

Source: Author page, Crossway · Author bio, First Things (May 2026). Last updated November 13, 2026.
