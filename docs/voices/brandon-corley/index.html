Brandon Corley

MDiv student at Southern Seminary | Writes for American Reformer on Baptist political theology and natural law

About

Brandon Corley is a graduate of The Master's University, where he earned a degree in Biblical Studies, and an MDiv student at The Southern Baptist Theological Seminary. He writes for American Reformer on Baptist political theology and natural law.

Source: Author page, American Reformer. Last updated November 12, 2026.

Where they write

  • American Reformer

Start here

  1. In Defense of the Baptist State
    American Reformer · June 19, 2024

    Argues that Baptists have no principled reason to reject an established Baptist religion, drawing on a 1659 declaration by Baptistic Congregationalists in London.

  2. On Baptist Establishment, Again
    American Reformer · June 30, 2025

    A follow-up that sets out the political principles behind the earlier essay and sketches what a Christian establishment could look like in America today.

  3. The Natural End of Man
    American Reformer · April 9, 2026

    Argues from the classical idea of natural ends that human nature is ordered to knowing and loving God, and considers natural virtue in that light.
