Karen Swallow Prior

Writer, former English professor and Religion News Service columnist | Author of The Evangelical Imagination and On Reading Well | Writes on reading, vocation, the evangelical imagination and Christian ethics

About

Karen Swallow Prior is a writer and speaker who spent decades as an English professor; she is a monthly columnist for Religion News Service, a contributing writer at The Dispatch, a senior fellow at the Trinity Forum and a research fellow at Comment. She holds a PhD from the State University of New York at Buffalo and a bachelor's degree from Daemen College, and her academic work centers on eighteenth century British literature. Her books include You Have a Calling (Brazos, 2025), The Evangelical Imagination (Brazos, 2023), On Reading Well (Brazos, 2018), Fierce Convictions (Thomas Nelson, 2014) and Booked (2012). She writes the newsletter The Priory on Substack and was named the 2025 to 2026 Karlson Scholar at Bethel Seminary.

  1. Only One Platform Will Last
    The Gospel Coalition · April 20, 2017

    Argues that the pursuit of blogs, branding and book deals seeks an affirmation only God can give, and that the platform that lasts is faithful work in one's calling within the church.

  2. The Birds and the Bees, Babies and Me
    Christianity Today · March 5, 2026

    Reflects on her own infertility, presents childlessness as a calling with biblical and historical examples, and argues that childless people should be more fully included in church life.

  3. What My Old Prayer Journal Taught Me
    Christianity Today · October 29, 2026

    Rereads a prayer journal kept 25 years earlier and traces how its record of answered prayers testifies to God's continuing faithfulness.

  4. Lessons from Loving and Losing a Pet
    Christianity Today · August 28, 2014

    Describes grieving her dog without the condolences given for a human death and argues that love and loss for animals reveal human frailty and God's love.
