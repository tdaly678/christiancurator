John Piper

Founder and lead teacher of Desiring God | Author of Desiring God: Meditations of a Christian Hedonist | Writes on Christian Hedonism, God's sovereignty and suffering

About

John Piper is the founder and lead teacher of Desiring God and chancellor of Bethlehem College and Seminary. He became senior pastor of Bethlehem Baptist Church in Minneapolis in 1980 and served there nearly 33 years, and since 2013 he has served full time with Desiring God. He studied English literature at Wheaton College, earned a Bachelor of Divinity at Fuller Theological Seminary and a doctorate from the University of Munich, and taught biblical studies at Bethel College in Saint Paul for six years. His books include Desiring God: Meditations of a Christian Hedonist (1987), The Pleasures of God, Future Grace and Don't Waste Your Life.

Source: Teacher profile, Desiring God · Author page, Crossway · Profile, The Gospel Coalition. Last updated November 12, 2026.

  1. We Want You to Be a Christian Hedonist!
    Desiring God · August 31, 2006

    Sets out Christian Hedonism in short form: God is most glorified in us when we are most satisfied in him.

  2. What We Believe About the Five Points of Calvinism
    Desiring God · March 1, 1985

    Explains the historical roots and biblical case for total depravity, irresistible grace, limited atonement, unconditional election and perseverance.

  3. Don’t Waste Your Cancer
    Desiring God · February 15, 2006

    Written on the eve of his prostate surgery, argues that God designs suffering for his people's good and lists ways a believer can waste or redeem it.

  4. What Does It Mean to Seek the Lord?
    Desiring God · August 19, 2009

    Explains seeking the Lord as seeking his manifest presence, and distinguishes that from God's constant omnipresence and covenant faithfulness.

    Desiring God · January 22, 2013
