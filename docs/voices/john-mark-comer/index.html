John Mark Comer

Founder of Practicing the Way | Author of The Ruthless Elimination of Hurry | Writes on spiritual formation, prayer and the practices of following Jesus

About

John Mark Comer is a writer, pastor and the founder of Practicing the Way, an organization working toward the renewal of apprenticeship to Jesus. For nearly two decades he was the founding pastor of Bridgetown Church in Portland, Oregon. He is the author of eight books, including Practicing the Way and The Ruthless Elimination of Hurry.
