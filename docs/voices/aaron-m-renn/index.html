Aaron M. Renn

Cofounder and senior fellow at American Reformer | Author of Life in the Negative World | Writes on evangelicalism, the negative world, men and leadership

About

Aaron M. Renn is cofounder and senior fellow at American Reformer and writes a newsletter at aaronrenn.com on religion, family, leadership, urbanism and technology. He is the author of Life in the Negative World: Confronting Challenges in an Anti-Christian Culture (Zondervan, 2024), which develops his "three worlds" model of Christianity's changing place in American life. He was previously a senior fellow in urban policy at the Manhattan Institute and a managing director at Accenture, and he lives in Indianapolis.

Source: About page, aaronrenn.com · Author page, American Reformer · Author bio, First Things. Last updated November 13, 2026.
