David VanDrunen

Robert B. Strimple Professor of Systematic Theology and Christian Ethics at Westminster Seminary California | Author of Politics after Christendom | Writes on natural law, the two kingdoms and Christian ethics

About

David VanDrunen is the Robert B. Strimple Professor of Systematic Theology and Christian Ethics at Westminster Seminary California, where he has taught since 2001, and a minister of the Orthodox Presbyterian Church who formerly pastored Grace Orthodox Presbyterian Church in Hanover Park, Illinois. He holds a BA from Calvin College, an MDiv from Westminster Seminary California, a ThM from Trinity Evangelical Divinity School, a JD from Northwestern University School of Law and a PhD from Loyola University Chicago. He is the author or editor of fifteen books, including Politics after Christendom, Divine Covenants and Moral Order, Living in God's Two Kingdoms and Natural Law: A Short Companion.

Source: Faculty page, Westminster Seminary California · Profile, The Gospel Coalition. Last updated November 12, 2026.

  1. How Two-Kingdoms Doctrine Helps Political Discourse
    The Gospel Coalition · March 30, 2022

    Argues that the Reformed two-kingdoms doctrine steers Christians between political quietism and triumphalism, treating public life as legitimate but temporary.

  2. The Christian’s Place in Society
    New Horizons · 2015-06

  3. The Reformation of Civil Government
    New Horizons · 2017-10

    Traces Reformed teaching on civil government from the Christendom model toward religious liberty and asks how believers should think about resistance as the culture grows more hostile.

  4. The Forgiveness of Sin
    The Gospel Coalition

    Explains how God's justice and mercy meet in Christ's substitutionary atonement so that sinners are forgiven by faith without compromise to God's justice.
