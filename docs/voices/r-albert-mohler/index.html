Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions about R. Albert Mohler

What denomination is Albert Mohler?

Albert Mohler is Southern Baptist. He has served as the ninth president of The Southern Baptist Theological Seminary in Louisville, Kentucky, the flagship seminary of the Southern Baptist Convention, since 1993, and he remains one of the SBC's most influential voices.

Is Albert Mohler a Calvinist?

Mohler identifies with Reformed soteriology within Baptist convictions and has been a key figure in the resurgence of Calvinism in the Southern Baptist Convention. Under his leadership, Southern Seminary returned to its founding confession, the Abstract of Principles, which reflects a Reformed Baptist theology.

What is The Briefing by Albert Mohler?

The Briefing is Mohler's daily podcast and program offering analysis of news and events from a Christian worldview. It is one of the most widely followed evangelical commentary programs on culture, politics, and theology.

What is Albert Mohler's view on women pastors?

Mohler is a complementarian who holds that the office and title of pastor are reserved for qualified men. At the 2026 SBC annual meeting he proposed a 'Truth and Unity Amendment' to the SBC constitution intended to clarify the Baptist Faith and Message 2000's position that women should not carry the title of pastor.

What is Albert Mohler known for?

Mohler is known for leading Southern Seminary's return to confessional orthodoxy after becoming its president at age 33, for his prolific cultural commentary, and for his role as a leading intellectual voice of conservative evangelicalism in America.
