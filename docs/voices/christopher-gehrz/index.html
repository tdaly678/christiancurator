Christopher Gehrz

Professor of history at Bethel University | Author of a religious biography of Charles Lindbergh | Writes for Mere Orthodoxy and The Pietist Schoolman on Christianity, history, and education

About

Christopher Gehrz is professor of history at Bethel University in St. Paul, Minnesota, where he has taught since 2003, and holds a PhD in history from Yale University. He is the author of Charles Lindbergh: A Religious Biography of America's Most Infamous Pilot (2021) and coauthor of The Pietist Option: Hope for the Renewal of Christianity (2017). He writes about Christianity, history, and education in his Substack newsletter, The Pietist Schoolman.

  1. Christians in the Age of Hitler
    Mere Orthodoxy · March 25, 2026

    Reviews Alec Ryrie's The Age of Hitler and How We Will Survive It, which holds that the Western moral consensus built on opposing Nazism is weakening, and asks what role Christians might play in what follows.

  2. The Moral Vision of American Leaders
    Current · July 30, 2024

    Reviews Marvin Olasky's Moral Vision, which uses short biographies of presidents to argue that character should guide voters, and questions both its omissions and its likely effect on committed voters.

  3. The Challenge of Graven Images
    Current · July 8, 2021

    Looks at the Charles Lindbergh statue at the Minnesota State Capitol and argues that historians should portray his full record, including his eugenics and antisemitism, while honoring the image of God in the people he dismissed.
