J. A. Medders

Director of theology and content, Send Network | Author of Humble Calvinism and Gospel Formed | Writes at Spiritual Theology on Spurgeon, the Puritans, and the Christian life

About

J. A. Medders is the director of theology and content for Send Network and the general editor for New Churches. He is also a fellow of The Spurgeon Library and visiting professor of Christian studies at Midwestern Baptist Theological Seminary. He earned a PhD in biblical spirituality at The Southern Baptist Theological Seminary under Donald S. Whitney. He is the author of Gospel Formed and Humble Calvinism, coauthor of The Soul-Winning Church, and writes the Spiritual Theology newsletter.
