Brian D. McLaren

Author, speaker and faculty member with the Center for Action and Contemplation | Author of A New Kind of Christian and Faith After Doubt | Writes on doubt, leaving evangelicalism, nationalism and the church

About

Brian D. McLaren describes himself as an author, speaker, activist and public theologian and is a former college English teacher and pastor. He is a faculty member with the Center for Action and Contemplation and cohosts its podcast Learning How to See. His books include A New Kind of Christian (2001), A Generous Orthodoxy, Faith After Doubt (2021), Do I Stay Christian? (2022) and Life After Doom (2024), and he has also written the novels The Last Voyage (2025) and The Great Rift (2026).

Source: About, brianmclaren.net · Books, brianmclaren.net · Author bio, Penguin Random House. Last updated November 13, 2026.

  1. Q & R: How did you transition out of Evangelicalism?
    brianmclaren.net · August 2, 2017

    Describes how he came to be seen as outside evangelicalism over LGBTQ inclusion, hell and penal substitution, and lists the practices he used for answering critics.

  2. Is Nationalism the Same as Patriotism?
    brianmclaren.net · July 3, 2017

    Distinguishes patriotism, a love for what is good in a country joined to a desire to improve it, from nationalism, which he argues seeks ultimate meaning in the nation and is therefore idolatrous.

  3. Q & R: A Young Person with Doubts?
    brianmclaren.net · August 21, 2018

    Answers a young believer by arguing that doubt is a sign of faith and a doorway to deeper questions, and points to several of his books for people leaving a conservative background.

  4. Why Pastors are Leaving the Church (Part 1)
    brianmclaren.net · April 18, 2017

    Draws on a pastor's letter to argue that clergy leave when congregations focus on internal religious matters and ask leaders to stay silent on moral and political questions.
