Jeremy Kimble

Professor of systematic and applied theology at Cedarville University | Author of Behold and Become | Writes for The Gospel Coalition and Christ Over All on Scripture, doctrine, and the church

About

Jeremy Kimble is professor of systematic and applied theology at Cedarville University and holds a PhD from Southeastern Baptist Theological Seminary. He is the author of Behold and Become: Reading Scripture for Transformation, How Can I Get More Out of My Bible Reading?, and 40 Questions About Church Membership and Discipline, and coauthor with Ched Spellman of Invitation to Biblical Theology. He served in pastoral ministry for eight years and is a member and minister at University Baptist Church.
