R.C. Sproul

Theologian and founder of Ligonier Ministries, 1939 to 2017 | Author of The Holiness of God and Chosen by God | Wrote on God's holiness, justification and the authority of Scripture

About

R.C. Sproul (1939 to 2017) founded Ligonier Ministries, which began in the 1970s as the Ligonier Valley Study Center in Ligonier, Pennsylvania, and moved to Orlando, Florida, in 1984. He was the first minister of preaching and teaching at Saint Andrew's Chapel in Sanford, Florida, the first president of Reformation Bible College and executive editor of Tabletalk magazine, and his Renewing Your Mind radio program still airs daily. He wrote more than one hundred books, including The Holiness of God, Chosen by God and Everyone's a Theologian, and edited the Reformation Study Bible (2015). He died on December 14, 2017, in Altamonte Springs, Florida, at age 78.

Source: Teacher page, Ligonier Ministries · Dr. R.C. Sproul, Called Home to the Lord, Ligonier Ministries · Who We Are, Ligonier Ministries. Last updated November 12, 2026.

  1. What Is Predestination?
    Ligonier Ministries

    Defines predestination and contrasts the prescient view, in which God elects on foreseen faith, with the Augustinian view, in which God's grace produces that faith.

  2. Is Scripture the Ultimate Authority?
    Ligonier Ministries

    Contrasts the Reformation principle of Scripture alone with Rome's view of tradition as a second source of revelation and argues the church recognized the canon rather than granting its authority.

  3. War and Peace with a Holy God
    Ligonier Ministries

    Argues from a childhood memory of VJ Day that justification by faith ends the sinner's hostility toward God and establishes a lasting peace kept by Christ the mediator.

  4. Justification by Death?
    Tabletalk · October 1, 2010

    Argues that justification by faith alone is essential to the gospel and challenges the popular assumption that everyone goes to heaven at death.

  5. A Letter to a Discouraged Pastor
    Tabletalk · April 1, 1992

    Answers a discouraged pastor from his own early struggles and urges faithful perseverance in loving the church Christ will present without spot or wrinkle.
