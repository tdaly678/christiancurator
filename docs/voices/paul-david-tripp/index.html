Paul David Tripp

Counselor & Author
Quick Facts
DenominationPresbyterian (longtime association with Tenth Presbyterian Church, Philadelphia)
TraditionReformed evangelical; biblical counseling movement
Current roleFounder and president of Paul Tripp Ministries
Known forGospel-centered books on marriage, parenting, and everyday sanctification, including New Morning Mercies and What Did You Expect?, drawn from decades in the biblical counseling movement.

Paul David Tripp (born November 12, 1950) is an American pastor, author, biblical counselor, and international conference speaker who has dedicated his career to connecting the transforming power of Jesus Christ to everyday life. After receiving his Master of Divinity from the Reformed Episcopal Seminary and Doctor of Ministry in Biblical Counseling from Westminster Theological Seminary, Tripp served as faculty at the Christian Counseling and Educational Foundation (CCEF) for many years.

Founder and president of Paul Tripp Ministries, a nonprofit organization producing gospel-centered resources, Tripp has authored more than 30 books including "What Did You Expect?: Redeeming the Realities of Marriage," "New Morning Mercies: A Daily Gospel Devotional," and "Do You Believe?: 12 Historic Doctrines to Change Your Everyday Life." His work exemplifies biblical counseling's maturation, combining theological depth with psychological insight to help Christians understand how grace transforms the ordinary struggles of daily life.

Frequently Asked Questions about Paul David Tripp

What denomination is Paul David Tripp?

Paul David Tripp comes from a Reformed and Presbyterian background. He trained at Reformed Episcopal Seminary and Westminster Theological Seminary and served for years on the pastoral staff of Tenth Presbyterian Church in Philadelphia. Today he ministers interdenominationally through Paul Tripp Ministries.

What is Paul David Tripp known for?

Tripp is best known as a biblical counselor and author of more than 30 books, including the bestselling daily devotional New Morning Mercies and the marriage book What Did You Expect?. His signature theme is connecting the transforming grace of Jesus Christ to the ordinary struggles of everyday life.

What is biblical counseling, and what is Tripp's role in it?

Biblical counseling is a movement that treats Scripture as the primary lens for understanding and addressing personal and relational problems. Tripp served on the faculty of the Christian Counseling and Educational Foundation (CCEF) for many years and is one of the movement's most widely read popularizers.

Is Paul David Tripp still in ministry?

Yes. Tripp, born in 1950, continues to lead Paul Tripp Ministries, a nonprofit that produces gospel-centered books, devotionals, and conference teaching for individuals, families, and pastors.
