Tony Reinke

Senior teacher at Desiring God and host of Ask Pastor John | Author of 12 Ways Your Phone Is Changing You | Writes on technology, joy and the Christian life

About

Tony Reinke is senior teacher and lead curator for Desiring God, which he joined in 2012, and the creator, producer and host of the Ask Pastor John podcast with John Piper. Before that he was editorial and research assistant to C.J. Mahaney at Sovereign Grace Ministries from 2008 to 2011. He has written seven books: Lit! A Christian Guide to Reading Books (2011), Newton on the Christian Life (2015), 12 Ways Your Phone Is Changing You (2017), The Joy Project (2018), Competing Spectacles (2019), God, Technology, and the Christian Life (2022) and Ask Pastor John (2024). He lives in Phoenix, Arizona, with his wife and family.

Start here

  1. Six Wrong Reasons to Check Your Phone in the Morning
    Desiring God · June 6, 2015

    Uses a Desiring God reader survey to name six habits behind reaching for the phone before God each morning and argues that what we first focus on shapes the whole day.

  2. Thirty Seconds Alone with God
    Desiring God · January 30, 2017

    Argues that attention is a gift from God that the attention economy competes for, and that the fight for prayer is fought in the short gaps of the day.

  3. Twelve Tips for Parenting in the Digital Age
    Desiring God · May 21, 2018

    Summarizes Jean Twenge's research on the iGen generation and offers parents practical counsel, including delaying smartphones and controlling home wifi.

  4. How Calvinists Miss the Key to Happiness
    Desiring God · April 3, 2017

    Argues that the five points of Calvinism should be connected to joy, and that the joy of Calvinism comes from experiencing God's sovereignty, not only knowing about it.

  5. Can Technology Delete Death?
    Desiring God · July 24, 2017

    Responds to Ray Kurzweil's transhumanist hope of overcoming death through technology and contrasts it with the Christian hope of immortality through trusting God.
