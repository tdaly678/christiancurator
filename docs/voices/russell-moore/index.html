Russell Moore

Editor at large and columnist at Christianity Today | Author of Losing Our Religion | Writes on faith and politics, Christian ethics and the evangelical church

About

Russell Moore is editor at large and a columnist at Christianity Today, where he was editor in chief from 2022 to 2025; he hosts The Russell Moore Show and co-hosts The Bulletin, CT's weekly news podcast. He was president of the Ethics & Religious Liberty Commission of the Southern Baptist Convention from 2013 to 2021, and before that served as provost and dean of the Southern Baptist Theological Seminary, where he taught theology and ethics. An ordained Baptist minister, he teaches the Bible at Immanuel Church in Nashville, and in April 2025 Lipscomb University named him its inaugural Bill and Crissy Haslam Endowed Distinguished Visiting Professor of Faith and Reason. His books include Losing Our Religion: An Altar Call for Evangelical America.

Source: About page, russellmoore.com · News release, Lipscomb University (April 29, 2025) · Writer page, Christianity Today. Last updated November 12, 2026.

    Argues that feeling estranged from one's political camp can prompt Christians to reexamine their loyalties and see themselves as pilgrims rather than partisans, following Jesus' refusal to join the factions of his day.

    Christianity Today · November 20, 2024

    Distinguishes cynical dark humor from laughter that resists numbness in grief, and draws on Peter Berger and Frederick Buechner to argue that darkness is real but not final.

  3. How I Changed My Mind (Kind Of) on the Death Penalty
    Christianity Today · October 21, 2026

  4. Why I Am Still a Christian
    Christianity Today · November 4, 2026

  5. Cultural Engagement
    Tabletalk · 2014-08
